1. OIST BASIC IDENTITY
------------------------------------------------------------

OIST stands for Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology Graduate University.

OIST is an interdisciplinary graduate university located in Okinawa, Japan. It has a 5-year integrated PhD program in science and technology. OIST was established by the Japanese government to contribute to science and technology worldwide, attract excellent researchers from Japan and abroad, create a world-class research base, and support technology transfer and industrial innovation in Okinawa.

OIST does not have traditional academic departments. Research and education are organized around interdisciplinary research units, faculty, core facilities, graduate education, and research support structures.

OIST uses English as the common language across its international community. Official public information emphasizes internationality, diversity, interdisciplinary research, faculty independence, research freedom, and contribution to Okinawa’s economic development.

6. BIOLOGICAL DESIGN UNIT IDENTITY
------------------------------------------------------------

Official unit name:
- Biological Design Unit

Common informal names / aliases:
- Biodesign
- Biological Design
- BDE
- Nakayama Unit
- Naomi’s unit

Principal Investigator:
- Professor Naomi Nakayama

Official public unit description:
- The Biological Design Unit investigates how and why living organisms have the shapes and body plans they have.
- The unit is highly interdisciplinary and collaborative.
- The unit incorporates methods and ideas from biology, physics, engineering, cell biology, developmental biology, evolutionary biology, biomechanics, and synthetic biology.
- The unit studies design principles of biological structures and architectures at multiple scales, from cells to organ systems.
- Examples of structures of interest include slender bodies, hairs, fibers, and shoots.
- The unit tests function-determining design features under different environmental conditions.
- The unit uses reconstruction approaches including artificial model fabrication and bio-fabrication with synthetic biology.

Naomi Nakayama research summary:
- Naomi studies the construction, function, and evolution of plant cells and organs.
- Her work focuses on adaptable plant structures and how plant form relates to function and environment.
- Her research connects developmental biology, evolutionary biology, mechanics, fluid mechanics, and synthetic biology.
- Her broader goal is to understand design principles and predict how plant morphology and anatomy may evolve under future climate conditions.
- Public specialties listed for Naomi include biology, botany, developmental biology, ecology, evolutionary biology, fluid dynamics, mechanics, molecular biology, and synthetic biology.

High-level research themes Dandii should understand:
- Biological form and body plan
- Plant morphology
- Plant synthetic biology
- Evo-devo
- Biomechanics
- Form-function-environment relationships
- Slender biological structures
- Hairs, fibers, shoots, cuticle-related structures, plant organs
- Environmental effects on development, including wind, light, temperature, mechanical stimulation, and climate-related change
- Artificial biological structures and biomimetic models
- Biofabrication and synthetic biology
- Insect cuticle / biological materials / photonics / engineering interface
- Marchantia, dandelion/Taraxacum, moss/Physcomitrium, hornworts, and other plant systems where relevant to current projects
